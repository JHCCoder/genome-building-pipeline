Supplemental Figure S7 — HiFi read-coverage analysis: paralogs vs single-copy genes

PURPOSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Distinguish genuine paralogs from uncollapsed haplotypes ("haplotigs") by
comparing PacBio HiFi read coverage. Genuine paralogs should show normal
diploid coverage (~36x for primary HiFi reads), whereas a haplotig — an
uncollapsed second copy of a single diploid locus — should show roughly HALF
(<= ~17-18x) coverage, because reads from one diploid locus are split between
two redundant assembled copies.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
METHODS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Locus definition and coverage.
  Regions were defined as each annotated gene body +/- 5 kb flanking sequence,
  for 26,260 nuclear genes (from the merged AGAT GFF,
  hifiasm-041425-denovoEnhanced_peaks2utr_sorted.agat.gff3) plus 12,428 BUSCO
  single-copy orthologs (glires_odb10), for a total of 38,688 regions. Mean
  per-region HiFi read depth was computed with mosdepth.

Per-chromosome normalization.
  Because the X and Y chromosomes are hemizygous in males, raw read depth on
  chrX and chrY is ~half the autosomal depth. To make coverage comparable
  across the genome, depth was normalized per chromosome by rescaling each
  chromosome class to the autosomal baseline:

      mean_depth_chr_norm = mean_depth * (C_autosomal / C_class)

  where C_autosomal is the genome-wide autosomal median depth (33.65x) and
  C_class is the median depth of that chromosome class (chrX = 19.47x, scale
  factor x1.728; chrY = 21.12x, scale factor x1.593). After normalization,
  chrX/chrY run at 33.65x, matching autosomes. (Small unplaced scaffolds
  seq31/seq200 were left uncorrected, consistent with the baseline-estimation
  routine.) The violin/density plots use mean_depth_chr_norm; the reference
  line is drawn at the 33.6x autosomal baseline.

Gene grouping (tiers).
  Genes were partitioned into four groups based on paralogy and overlap with
  segmental duplications (segdups), plus BUSCO orthologs as an independent
  single-copy control:

      G1 / T4 = singleton, NOT in segdup   (n = 15,330)  cleanest single-copy control
      G2 / T1 = paralog,  NOT in segdup    (n =    533)
      G3 / T3 = singleton, in segdup       (n =  8,749)
      G4 / T2 = paralog,  in segdup        (n =  1,648)
      BUSCO   = single-copy orthologs      (n = 12,428)  independent control

  Paralogs were defined by the strict copy-number rule (base gene name
  appearing >1x in the annotation): 2,181 paralog copies in 1,091 families.

Statistical tests.
  Two-sided Mann-Whitney U tests with Cliff's delta (delta) effect size, on
  mean_depth_chr_norm. Significance: * p<0.05, ** p<0.01, *** p<0.001,
  **** p<0.0001.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RESULTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Summary statistics (mean_depth_chr_norm):

  Group            N        Median   Mean    Std     Q1      Q3
  ----            ----      ------   ----    ---     ---     ---
  G2 paralog      533       34.64    34.79   9.24    29.12   40.54
  G4 paralog+seg  1,648     35.81    35.80   8.82    29.89   41.42
  G3 singleton+seg 8,749    36.04    35.74   7.64    30.62   41.06
  G1 singleton    15,330    32.72    33.27   8.24    27.32   38.83
  BUSCO           12,428    32.72    33.11   7.76    27.41   38.70

Pairwise comparisons (Mann-Whitney U, Cliff's delta):

  G1 (singleton) vs G2 (paralog), both outside segdups:
      p = 9.12e-06 (****),  delta = +0.113
      -> paralogs are slightly but significantly HIGHER than single-copy controls

  G3 (singleton) vs G4 (paralog), both within segdups:
      p = 0.555 (ns),  delta = +0.009
      -> within segmental duplications, paralog and singleton coverage are
         indistinguishable

  (G1+G2) outside segdups vs (G3+G4) within segdups:
      p = 1.14e-145 (****),  delta = -0.187
      -> genes inside segmental duplications have higher coverage than those
         outside, consistent with true duplication

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
INTERPRETATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Paralogs show FULL diploid HiFi coverage, not the ~half coverage expected of
uncollapsed haplotypes:

  - Paralogs (G2 and G4) run at mean 34.8x and 35.8x, respectively — at or
    slightly ABOVE the 33.6x autosomal baseline and ABOVE the single-copy
    controls (G1 = 33.3x, BUSCO = 33.1x). A haplotig would instead be expected
    at <= ~17x (half coverage).

  - Paralogs outside segmental duplications are actually slightly (but
    significantly) HIGHER in coverage than single-copy controls (delta = +0.11,
    p = 9.1e-06) — the opposite of the haplotig prediction.

  - No paralog group shows reduced coverage. The elevation of coverage within
    segmental-duplication regions (delta = -0.19) is consistent with these
    being genuine duplicated sequence.

CONCLUSION: the annotated paralog set represents genuine duplicated loci, not
uncollapsed haplotypes. Haplotype-collapse artifacts would be visible as
~half-depth coverage, which is not observed for any paralog group.
