We define the segdup pervasive region as the minimum number of segdup regions
that make up 80% of the total segdup lengths (Figure 7A). Using this criterion,
we identified the 1,426 longest segdup regions containing 417.9 Mbp of sequence,
which includes 1,271 gene features equating to 469 unique, non-LOC, genes
(Figure 7A). GO enrichment analysis of the 469 genes revealed significant
enrichment for pathways related to apoptosis (mitochondrial outer membrane
permeabilization; 3/5 genes, padj = 0.038; GO:0097345), nuclear organization
(regulation of protein localization to Cajal body; 4/9 genes, padj = 0.017;
GO:1904869, GO:1904871), metabolic processes (retinol metabolic process:
5/18 genes, padj = 0.023; GO:0042572; primary alcohol metabolic process:
8/37 genes, padj = 0.006; GO:0034308), and immune responses, particularly
interferon signaling and host defense (cellular response to type II interferon:
8/38 genes, padj = 0.006; GO:0071346; response to type II interferon: 8/48
genes, padj = 0.017; GO:0034341; defense response to virus: 16/161 genes,
padj = 0.006; GO:0051607; defense response to bacterium: 14/165 genes,
padj = 0.037; GO:0042742). We wondered whether the gene features enriched in
the GO terms are transcriptionally active and decided to examine the genes
enriched in the term Regulation of Protein Localization to Cajal Body pathway.
Specifically, the gene features found in this pathway were: Cct3-l1, Cct4-dl2,
Cct7-dl3, Larp7-dl2, Larp7-dl4, Larp7-dl5. These genes are transferred from the
OctDeg1.0 annotation via Liftoff and renamed (Cct3-l1, renamed from LOC101561532)
or potential "paralogs" added from the de novo braker annotation that could be
functional paralogs or processed pseudogenes (Cct4-dl2, Cct7-dl3, Larp7-dl2,
Larp7-dl4, Larp7-dl5). Looking beyond the segdup pervasive region, we found a
total of 21 gene features related to these 4 genes, all of which lie within
segdup regions. Specifically, we found Cct7, Cct4, Cct3, and Larp7 to have 8, 3,
4, and 6 paralogous genes, respectively. We then visualized all the associated
segdup links and positions of these genes (Cct3, Cct4, Cct7, Larp7) and their
corresponding gene features (Figure 7D). We mapped degu transcriptomic data from
29 tissues across five organs (NCBI BioProject PRJNA788430) to determine whether
the 21 gene features are transcriptionally active. The results show detectable
expression of at least one feature for each of the four genes examined
(Figure 7F). These data indicate that segmentally duplicated genes in the degu
genome are enriched for functionally active genes involved in Cajal body
function.
